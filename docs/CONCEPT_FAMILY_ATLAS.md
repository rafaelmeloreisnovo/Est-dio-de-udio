# Concept Family Atlas

This atlas keeps related ideas together without collapsing distinct evidence layers.

## A. Sound source and capture

```text
mechanical wave
-> pressure variation
-> microphone transduction
-> ADC
-> PCM
```

Families: waveform, sample rate, channels, phase, polarity, peak, RMS, noise, clipping.

## B. Spectral analysis

Families: Goertzel bands, FFT/STFT future route, spectrogram, transfer response, phase, coherence, harmonics, THD.

## C. Room / electroacoustics

Families: excitation, sync, sweep, latency, impulse response, ETC/EDC, EDT/T20/T30, reverberation, constrained room correction.

## D. Spatial sound

Families: mono/stereo, channel matrix, ambience, future ambisonic/spatial encodings, speaker geometry and transforms.

## E. Voice

Families: energy, zero crossings, periodicity, pitch candidate, formants future route, phonetic features, timing, time-map, formant/pitch separation.

## F. Processing

Families: HPF, gate, adaptive LMS, parametric biquad bank, leveler, limiter, normalization, convolution, A/B and bypass.

## G. Containers and provenance

Families: ZRF, CFR, typed chunks, raw source preservation, SHA-256, ABI manifest, CI receipt, installation evidence.

## H. Device / hardware

Families: microphone capability, speaker path, Android audio properties, accelerometer, sensor inventory, μ∆ vibration observation, ABI/CPU.

## I. Research representations

Matrices, vector spaces, Poincaré-derived or higher-dimensional feature representations may be used as analytical coordinate systems. Their presence does not by itself establish a physical mechanism.

## Cross-family invariant

```text
representation != measurement
measurement != diagnosis
correlation != causation
implementation != validation
```
