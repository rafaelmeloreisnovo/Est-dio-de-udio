# Rafaelia Audio Studio — Product Overview

## Purpose

A mobile audio workstation combining recording, narration, DSP, measurement, reproducible evidence and research-oriented signal representations.

## Main workspaces

`REC | EDIT | CAL | SPEC | ROOM | VOICE | MASTER | EXPORT`

## Differentiators

- raw audio preservation before DSP;
- fixed-point freestanding C cores;
- ARMv7 and AArch64 zero-undefined gates;
- ZRF session and CFR calibration containers;
- relative speaker-room-microphone capture;
- evidence bundle generated on the installed phone;
- μ∆ accelerometer observation for vibration/motion context;
- fail-closed claim states instead of invented calibration data.

## Boundaries

Android APIs remain necessary for screen, microphone, speaker, storage, codecs and sensors.
Absolute SPL is unavailable until a physical acoustic reference is supplied.
Sensor vibration is an observation layer, not automatic fault diagnosis.
